# Foundations

Foundations covers the core concepts behind AI engineering: what the field is,
how language models work, tokens and context windows, embeddings, and how to
drive models with prompts and structured outputs. Start here before moving into
integration, retrieval, and production topics.

- [What is AI Engineering?](what-is-ai-engineering.md): the field, its scope,
  and how it differs from adjacent disciplines.
- [LLM Fundamentals](llm-fundamentals.md): how language models work, model
  selection, and common failure modes.
- [Tokens and Context Windows](tokens-and-context-windows.md): tokenization,
  context limits, and sampling parameters.
- [Embeddings](embeddings.md): vector representations and semantic similarity.
- [Prompt Engineering](prompt-engineering.md): instructions, examples,
  constraints, and prompt versioning.
- [Structured Outputs](structured-outputs.md): schemas, validation, and typed
  application contracts.
