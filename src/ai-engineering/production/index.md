# Production

Production covers shipping and operating AI features reliably: observing model
and system behavior, managing cost and latency, caching, guardrails, safety and
security, deployment, and the MLOps/LLMOps lifecycle.

- [Observability](observability.md): traces, token usage, retrieval quality, and
  quality alerts.
- [Cost Optimization](cost-optimization.md): routing, caching, and measuring
  cost per successful task.
- [Latency Optimization](latency-optimization.md): streaming, parallelism, and
  perceived latency.
- [Caching](caching.md): deterministic results, cache keys, TTLs, and when
  caching is risky.
- [Guardrails](guardrails.md): moderation, schema-constrained outputs, and real
  defenses.
- [Safety and Security](safety-and-security.md): prompt injection, data
  leakage, and red-teaming.
- [Deployment](deployment.md): release strategy, rollback planning, and degraded
  modes.
- [MLOps and LLMOps](mlops-llmops.md): versioning, experiment tracking, and
  governance.
