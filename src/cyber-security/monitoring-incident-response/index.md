# Monitoring and Incident Response

Monitoring and incident response is about detecting suspicious behavior,
understanding what happened when something goes wrong, and responding safely
and quickly.

This section covers logging, detection tooling, and the structured response
flow used when an incident occurs.

- [Logging](logging.md): which security-relevant events to log and what to keep
  out of logs.
- [Security Monitoring](security-monitoring.md): continuous collection,
  baselining, and actionable alerts.
- [SIEM](siem.md): aggregating and correlating logs and alerts across systems.
- [IDS / IPS](ids-ips.md): detecting and blocking suspicious traffic.
- [Incident Response](incident-response.md): the response flow from detect to
  review.
- [Forensics Basics](forensics-basics.md): preserving evidence without
  destroying it.

See also [Security Architecture](../security-architecture/index.md) for the
principles that shape what you detect and how.
